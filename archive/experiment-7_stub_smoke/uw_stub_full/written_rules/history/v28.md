# House rule book
- A hazardous occupation combined with a hazardous avocation takes an exclusion rider for the avocation, not a flat extra and not debits.
- Sleep apnea with documented CPAP compliance carries no debit.
- Mildly elevated liver enzymes with no alcohol history carry no debit.
- A large face amount at a high multiple of income is postponed for financial evidence rather than rated.
- Several cardiac risk factors appearing together carry an interaction charge on top of the individual debits.
- A recent DUI is postponed, not rated.
- Private aviation at a modest number of hours a year takes a flat extra rather than rating debits, and is never declined for the flying alone.
- Adult-onset diabetes first diagnosed in later middle age, with the A1c well controlled, earns a credit against the debits.
- Weight lost in the past year is added back at half before the build band is read.
- Family-history debits are dropped once the applicant is older than the family-history qualifying age.
- Blood pressure that is treated and controlled carries the treatment charge only; the reading is not charged on top.
- Where the total cholesterol band and the ratio band disagree, the ratio governs; the cholesterol band is not charged.
