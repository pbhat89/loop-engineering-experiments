# House rule book
- A hazardous occupation combined with a hazardous avocation takes an exclusion rider for the avocation, not a flat extra and not debits.
- Sleep apnea with documented CPAP compliance carries no debit.
- Mildly elevated liver enzymes with no alcohol history carry no debit.
- A large face amount at a high multiple of income is postponed for financial evidence rather than rated.
- Several cardiac risk factors appearing together carry an interaction charge on top of the individual debits.
- A recent DUI is postponed, not rated.
