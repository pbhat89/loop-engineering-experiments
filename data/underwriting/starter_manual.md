# Individual life underwriting manual (starter edition)

This is the whole of the written practice. Rate every application from it. Where it says *refer to underwriting judgement*, the manual has nothing more to give you.

## 1. Decision vocabulary

Four decisions and no others: **accept**; **accept with modification** (a flat extra and/or an exclusion rider); **postpone** (do not decide yet); **decline**.

## 2. Rating classes and the debit bands

Add the debits from sections 3-14 and read the class off the total: 0-49 Standard | 50-99 Table 2 | 100-149 Table 4 | 150-199 Table 6 | 200-249 Table 8 | 250+ Decline.

A decline band in any single table declines the file whatever the total comes to. Three preferred classes sit above Standard. Each needs **zero debits** and, in addition:

- **Standard Plus**: BMI 27.9 or under; BP 138/88 or under and untreated; TC 240 or under with ratio 5.0 or under; A1c 6.4 or under with no diabetes diagnosis; tobacco never or quit over 5 yr; 0 moving violations and no DUI in 5 yr; occupation class A or B; no hazardous avocation; at most 1 family-history event; no sleep apnea, no alcohol history, normal liver chemistry.
- **Preferred**: BMI 27.0 or under; BP 134/84 or under and untreated; TC 220 or under with ratio 4.5 or under; A1c 6.0 or under with no diabetes diagnosis; tobacco never or quit over 5 yr; 0 moving violations and no DUI ever; occupation class A; no hazardous avocation; no qualifying family history; no sleep apnea, no alcohol history, normal liver chemistry.
- **Preferred Plus**: BMI 26.0 or under; BP 128/80 or under and untreated; TC 200 or under with ratio 4.0 or under; A1c 5.6 or under with no diabetes diagnosis; tobacco never; 0 moving violations and no DUI ever; occupation class A; no hazardous avocation; no qualifying family history; no sleep apnea, no alcohol history, normal liver chemistry.

Zero debits meeting none of the three is **Standard**.

## 3. Build

Height, weight and BMI are on the file. Read the BMI: up to 18.9 25 | 19.0-27.9 0 | 28.0-30.9 25 | 31.0-33.9 50 | 34.0-36.9 100 | 37.0-39.9 150 | 40.0+ decline.

For a recent change in build, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 4. Blood pressure

Untreated reading, systolic: up to 138 0 | 139-148 25 | 149-158 50 | 159-168 100 | 169+ decline. Diastolic: up to 88 0 | 89-94 25 | 95-99 50 | 100-104 100 | 105+ decline. Charge the worse of the two bands. On treatment: 25.

For a treated condition whose reading is now at target, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 5. Lipids

Total cholesterol: up to 240 0 | 241-280 25 | 281-320 50 | 321+ 75. Total cholesterol to HDL ratio: up to 5.0 0 | 5.1-6.5 25 | 6.6+ 50. Both tables are in force.

Where two tables in this manual point at different bands, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 6. Diabetes and A1c

A1c: up to 6.4 0 | 6.5-6.9 50 | 7.0-7.9 75 | 8.0-8.9 125 | 9.0+ decline. Type 2 diabetes is rated on the A1c. The year of diagnosis and any medication are on the file.

For late-onset metabolic disease that is well controlled, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 7. Tobacco

Never used, or quit more than 5 years ago: non-smoker, 0. Quit 1 to 5 years ago: 25. Current use: smoker rates - in this manual the smoker class runs on the same debit ladder, so add 75 and cap the best available class at Standard. A nicotine-positive laboratory result against a declared *never* makes the applicant a current user.

## 8. Family history

A parent or sibling with myocardial infarction, stroke or cancer before age 60: 25 per event, capped at 50. The applicant's own age does not change the charge.

For applicants well past the family-history qualifying age, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 9. Occupation

Class A (0): office administrator, regional sales manager, software engineer, schoolteacher, pharmacist. Class B (25): delivery driver, site foreman, welder, commercial fisher's deckhand, warehouse supervisor. Class C (50, hazardous): structural steel erector, underground miner, offshore rig hand, logging feller, high-voltage lineman.

## 10. Avocations

For private aviation, refer to underwriting judgement. Where no referral is available, rate private aviation as a hazardous avocation: 50. Scuba, by maximum depth: up to 60 ft 0 | 61-100 ft 25 | 101+ ft 50. Technical rock or ice climbing: 50. Amateur motorsport competition: a flat extra of $5.00 per $1,000.

Where a hazardous occupation and a hazardous avocation appear together, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 11. Driving record

Moving violations in the last 3 years: 0-1 0 | 2 25 | 3+ 50. A DUI conviction: rate 50.

For a recent motoring conviction, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 12. Alcohol and liver

Elevated liver enzymes: 25. A history of treatment for alcohol use: 50.

For borderline liver chemistry, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 13. Sleep apnea

Diagnosed sleep apnea: 50.

For treated sleep-disordered breathing, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 14. Financial underwriting

The face amount applied for, plus cover already in force, should sit within the income multiple for the applicant's age: 18-30 30x | 31-40 25x | 41-50 20x | 51-60 15x | 61+ 10x.

Where the face amount is large relative to income, refer to underwriting judgement.

## 15. Modifiers

A flat extra is charged per $1,000 of face amount in one of three bands: $2.50 / $5.00 / $7.50. An exclusion rider removes one named hazard from cover and carries no charge; name the hazard excluded. Either modifier makes the decision *accept with modification*.

## 16. Where this manual stops

For combinations of cardiac risk factors, refer to underwriting judgement. A referral line means the manual has reached the edge of what it can tell you. Rate the file on what the tables do say, and expect the reviewer to correct you.
